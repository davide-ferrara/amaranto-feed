# Team crests

- `vigor_lamezia.png`: unchanged 128px crest from the [official Vigor Lamezia site](https://www.vigorlamezia.it/), [source image](https://www.vigorlamezia.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Vigor-Logo-128x128.png). Used for team identification because the LND-supplied image is faded. Club marks remain the property of their respective owners.
- `trapani.png`: unchanged crest from the [official FC Trapani site](https://trapanicalcio.com/), [source image](https://trapanicalcio.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Trapani-Club-108x128.png). Replaces the off-center LND image.

Calendar and standings crests share a white padded tile so transparent artwork
and images containing a white background are presented consistently in both themes.
